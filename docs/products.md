# Products

A product is something a company sells. Products are managed from the core web app (`/products`) and from the mobile app (**Productos** tab). Core is the source of truth for validation, identifiers, prices, stock, images and persistence; mobile adapts navigation and controls but follows the same rules through `/api/products`.

Code: `apps/core/src/features/products/` (core), `apps/mobile/src/features/products/` (mobile). JSON contracts: `shared/contracts/products.ts`. Editor rules shared by web and mobile: `shared/product-variants.ts` and, for photos, `shared/product-photos.ts`. Schema: `Product`, `ProductPhoto`, `ProductOption`, `ProductOptionValue`, `ProductVariant`, `ProductVariantOptionValue` and `ProductStock` in `apps/core/prisma/schema.prisma`.

## Model

- A product has a name, an optional description, up to 8 ordered photos (see [Photos](#photos) and [Images](images.md)), a currency, up to 3 ordered options and at least one variant.
- An option has a name and an ordered list of values («Talla»: S, M, L). A variant is one value of each option; its **combination** is written in option order and separated by « / » («L / Azul»). A product without options has exactly one variant with an empty combination.
- A variant has an optional SKU, a sale price, an optional purchase price, exactly one stock record, a status (`active` or `inactive`) and an immutable QR identifier. The QR is separate from the internal id and the SKU; it is what labels print and what [message-to-order](message-to-order.md) reads from photos.
- The product also has an immutable QR identifier, a structure version that increases on every structural write (see [Concurrency](#concurrency)) and a stored visibility, `hidden` (see [Hiding a product](#hiding-a-product)).
- Options and values have their own rows, and each variant links to one value per option. Reason: the order is stored, and renaming a value renames it in all its variants at once (rejected: deducing the options from free per-variant attributes, which can store neither).
- Options, values and variants keep the order the seller gave them; by default, the order in which she typed them. That order is used by the product screens, the variants table, the combination label, the print sheet and label, «Nueva venta», the [public catalog](public-catalog.md), the [checkout](checkout.md) and new [orders](orders.md). Reason: S, M, L is not alphabetical.
- A product without options has one variant, always active, and no variant detail page; its SKU, prices and stock are in the product form.
- Photos are rows of their own (`ProductPhoto`, one per product and position 0 to 7, each pointing to a company image); the primary is position 0. Reason: each photo keeps a foreign key to the company's images and search joins photos to embeddings by index (rejected: an array of image ids on the product, which has neither). The image reference restricts deletion, so a future image cleanup cannot delete a photo in use. The migration that introduced the table turned each product's single photo into its only and primary photo, without seller action. It has no automated test, because the [testing skill](../.agents/skills/testing/SKILL.md) forbids migration tests; it is checked by hand when written.
- There is no product deletion, archiving or category. A product is withdrawn from buyers by hiding it (see [Hiding a product](#hiding-a-product)); it stops being sold altogether, for the seller too, only by deactivating all its variants, which a product without options cannot do.

## Rules

| Aspect | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Any authenticated user of the company can create, view and edit its products. The company comes from the session, never from the request. A product or variant of another company behaves as non-existent, also in the variant detail and in **Cambiar combinación**. |
| Name | Required, up to 200 characters. Duplicate names are allowed. |
| Description | Optional, up to 5,000 characters. |
| Limits | 3 options per product, 30 values per option, 100 variants per product, and 1–30 characters per option or value name after trimming. Reason: they cover clothing and footwear and keep the table and the catalog manageable. They are constants of the shared contract so the editors apply them without a round trip; core validates them again on the final state and on the lists the client sent. |
| Variant count | The 100 counts active, inactive and unsaved new variants, not those marked for removal or deletion. Creating generates every combination, so the editor rejects a product of value counts above 100 (5 × 6 × 4 = 120); on a saved product it rejects existing plus generated variants above 100. Adding an option to a large product can therefore be blocked even if the seller meant to remove most new combinations; this is an accepted cost of the single limit. |
| Option and value names | Required. Two options, or two values of one option, cannot match ignoring case and surrounding spaces («m» and «M » are the same value). |
| Combination | A variant has exactly one value of each option. Two variants of a product, active or inactive, cannot have the same combination. Not every combination must exist: the seller removes the ones she does not sell. |
| SKU | Optional, up to 100 characters, never generated or copied between variants. Unique per company ignoring case and surrounding spaces (database constraint); many variants may have none. When two rows of one save carry the same SKU, both are rejected. Reason: the QR identifies what is sold; the SKU is the seller's own data. |
| Prices | Per variant. Sale price greater than zero; purchase price optional and zero or more. Two decimals at most, maximum 999,999,999.99 in the product currency. Excess precision is rejected, never rounded. A save with a variant without sale price is rejected and the rows are marked. Prices are shown and edited with two decimals. |
| Currency | The client sends the currency explicitly, taken from the company's country (PE→PEN, US→USD, CO→COP, AR→ARS, CL→CLP, BR→BRL). Core validates the code. It cannot be edited. Reason: the contract allows a currency selector later without changing the API. |
| Stock | Per variant. The initial stock (zero or more, default zero) is set only when the variant is created, in a new product or as a «Nueva» row of a saved one. Afterwards users cannot edit it; it changes only when an order deducts it (the automatic deduction of an order due for it, shipping, or creation with immediate delivery or shipment) and when a cancelled order that had deducted it restores it. A restoration is a stock increase: in the same transaction it deducts the waiting orders it covers, oldest first ([Orders · Stock deduction](orders.md#stock-deduction)). The product shows the sum of its active variants, read-only. Reason: with options, price, stock and SKU live in one place, the variant. |
| QR | One per variant, generated by core when saved and never changed by renaming, reordering, changing the combination, deactivating or reactivating. Reason: printed labels and orders keep pointing to the same variant. |
| Base values on create | When the first option is added, the generated combinations copy the sale price, purchase price and initial stock typed before (an empty stock counts as 0), never the SKU. Reason: the seller does not type the same thing six times. If the copied stock is above zero, a notice says stock is per combination until the first stock edit or the save. |
| Option changes before saving | An unsaved combination is identified by its values, not its text: renaming or reordering keeps everything edited. Adding a value does not change the existing combinations. Adding an option gives existing combinations its first value and keeps what was edited in them; the combinations with its other values copy the prices of the combination they come from, the base stock and an empty SKU. Removing one option of several keeps the combinations with its first value, with what was edited in them. |
| Renaming | Renaming an option or value renames it in all its variants. Orders and checkouts already created keep the text they copied, and new ones use the new name; printed labels keep the old name and their QR keeps working. Reason: an order describes what was agreed. No history of former names is kept: «Antes «Azul»» is only shown while editing. |
| Values without variants | Every save (editor, change combination, delete variant) drops from the option any value left with no variant, counting inactive ones, and then any option left with no values. The value is dropped, not rejected; change combination and delete say so in their confirmation. |
| Adding a value | Creates only the combinations that contain it, never the ones the seller removed. A new combination copies the sale and purchase price of the first variant, in current order, that shares its other values, preferring an active one and otherwise an inactive one; if none shares them, the product's first variant; in a new product without other combinations, the base values. It starts with stock 0 in a saved product (the base stock in a new one) and no SKU. A value created while changing one variant's combination creates no other combination. |
| Adding an option | Existing variants, active or inactive, receive the value the seller chose and keep their id, QR, SKU, prices, stock, status and orders. Reason: labels and orders keep pointing to the same variant (rejected: splitting existing variants). The other combinations are created as new variants and copy the prices of the variant they come from. A product without options may receive its first option the same way. |
| New variants | Always created active, even when copied from an inactive one; the table warns before saving. |
| Changing a combination | Allowed even if the variant has orders or is inactive, as long as it does not repeat another variant's combination. Reason: it fixes data-entry mistakes without losing the variant. Orders keep their copy. It is saved at once; a new value is appended at the end of its option. |
| Removing (unsaved) | Removing combinations, values or options not yet saved is not deleting: they are not created and need no condition. |
| Deleting a variant | Only if it never appeared in any order, its stock is 0 and it is not the product's only variant. Otherwise it is deactivated, never deleted. Reason: order history, counted goods and printed labels are not lost. The variant, its QR and its zero stock are removed. Unconfirmed catalog orders count as orders even though the seller cannot see them; those without payments are deleted after 7 days ([Public catalog](public-catalog.md)), and the variant can then be deleted. |
| Deleting a value | Deletes all its variants, so each one must meet the deletion conditions. The editor checks it when the trash icon is tapped and core checks it again on save. |
| Deleting an option | Only an option with a single value; the variants lose that attribute and keep everything else. If it was the last option the remaining variant must be active and the product becomes a product without options. |
| Deactivating | Only for products with options, including the last active variant. Reason: it is the only way to stop selling a product with options altogether, since products cannot be archived; hiding only withdraws a product from buyers. An inactive variant leaves the public catalog, «Nueva venta», QR and photo lookups of message-to-order, and the «Desde» price and total stock of the list. It keeps its QR, SKU, prices, stock and orders; with stock above zero the seller is warned, not blocked. **Activar variante** returns it with its stock, without confirmation. Photo search only adds a product with exactly one active variant, so a product left with one active variant becomes recognizable by photo ([Image product search](image-product-search.md)). |
| Orders with an inactive variant | Orders that already contain it keep working: they are shown, paid (stock is deducted, also automatically on restock while the order waits for stock) and cancelled (stock is restored), including an unconfirmed catalog order the buyer reopens with **Continuar**, which can be confirmed and paid; the seller settles it in the chat. No new order can be created with it from web, mobile, the catalog or message-to-order; the item is marked «ya no está disponible». |
| Product without active variants | Not offered by the catalog or «Nueva venta» and not recognized by message-to-order. It stays in the list marked «Sin variantes activas» and returns to sale when any variant is activated or added. Deactivating variants is independent of hiding: a hidden product keeps its variants' status and a hiding never deactivates anything. |
| Hidden product | See [Hiding a product](#hiding-a-product). |
| Data copied into orders | The product name, the combination (names and values in option order at that moment), the SKU and the price. Later edits, renames, reorders, deactivations or deletions never change an order or a checkout. Everything else (product page, catalog, «Nueva venta», print sheet, new labels) shows the current options, values, order, prices and status. |
| Parity | Web and mobile apply the same rules, limits, texts and error codes; core is the source of truth. Reason: the seller works from both. |
| Concurrency | See [Concurrency](#concurrency). |

### Stock levels

A variant's stock has three levels: «Agotado» (0 units), «Stock bajo» (1 to 3 units) and available (4 or more). They apply to one active variant, never to a product total. The threshold is one constant for every store, changed only by Yoyos (`features/products/domain/stock-level.ts`). A product is «Agotado» only when every active variant is at 0, as in the list and the public catalog. The seller screens do not filter by «stock bajo» yet; the [AI assistants](assistants.md#stock-levels) filter and summarize with these levels. Reason: one meaning of «stock bajo» for every surface that adds it.

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Seller can | Seller sees | Buyer sees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active | On sale | Edit, sell, change the combination, deactivate; delete under the deletion conditions | The variant with its price and stock | The combination, selectable when in stock |
| Inactive | No longer sold, kept | View, edit prices and SKU from the detail, print its QR, change the combination, activate; delete under the same conditions | «Desactivada» in the product page, the variants list and the print sheet | Nothing in the catalog; in an existing order, the item as before |

Deleting is not a status: the variant stops existing. «Nueva», «Se quitará» and «Se eliminará» are marks of unsaved changes, not statuses.

## Screens

Web and mobile apply the same rules, limits and error codes. Mobile creates and edits on full screens without the tab bar. Controls (chips, **Editar**, row controls) are at least 44 px tall on mobile and 32 px on web.

- **List:** newest first; name (a hidden product carries the mark «Oculto»), SKU («Varias variantes» when there are several, «Sin SKU» when absent), sale price («Desde» the minimum of the active variants when prices differ) and total stock of the active variants. A product without active variants shows «Sin variantes activas» instead of the price. A hidden product shows the text mark «Oculto» with an eye-off icon next to its name, never color alone (web: the neutral badge; mobile: a small text mark with the icon on its own line under the stock line, so a long name still wraps and the price column does not move), with an accessible name that adds «oculto» and a contrast of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark; one without active variants shows both marks. Order, search and pages do not change, and a hidden product is found by name and SKU like any other. Search is partial and case-insensitive over the product name and any variant SKU; each product appears once. Pages of 20 (web numbered pages, mobile **Cargar más**). An empty catalog, a search without results and a load failure are distinct states.
- **Create:** name, description, photos, the «Ocultar producto» switch (off by default), SKU, sale price, purchase price and initial stock. **Agregar opciones como talla o color** opens the option editor. Once there are options, the generated combinations copy the price and initial stock typed before (never the SKU), the product form stops showing SKU, prices and stock («Stock» becomes the read-only «Suma del stock de las variantes»), and the seller adjusts rows one by one or in bulk by value. Combinations she does not sell are removed before saving. Saving opens the product. Removing the only option of an unsaved product is the one removal that asks first («¿Quitar las opciones?», **Quitar opciones** / **Conservar opciones**), because it changes the form's shape; confirming restores SKU, prices and stock as they were before adding options.
- **Option editor** (web inside the Variants card, mobile «Opciones» screen): option name, ordered values (one per row, each with a move handle and a trash icon, the last empty field «Agregar otro valor»), **Eliminar** and **Listo**. At 3 options **Agregar otra opción** is replaced by «Llegaste al máximo de 3 opciones.»; an option marked for removal counts until saved or undone. On web, collapsed options and the option being edited are separated by a divider, not boxed. Errors appear next to the field with the text intact and disable that option's **Listo**; over 100 variants the editor explains the count («serían 120 combinaciones (5 tallas × 6 colores × 4 mangas)», or «104 variantes (98 actuales y 6 nuevas)»). A new option on a saved product asks for the value existing variants receive. On mobile the header **Listo** applies every option and is disabled while any has errors or the limit is exceeded, the card **Listo** closes one option, and **X** leaves without applying.
- **Product page** (`/products/:productId` on web): the same Variants card as creation. **Guardar cambios** (web) or **Guardar** (mobile) saves everything edited on the page together: options, values, order, new variants with their SKU, removals and prices. It saves all or nothing. Stock of saved variants is read-only. Without active variants it shows «Ninguna variante está a la venta…». A product without options keeps today's form; on mobile its QR and printing stay on the product page.
- **Variants table** (web table in the card, mobile «Variantes» screen): on web it is a subsection of the Variants card, not a nested card: a single divider above it separates it from the options, and that divider is dropped while an option is being edited so only one rule shows there. The subsection opens with the heading «Variantes del producto» and, right below it, the counter; the value chips, **Todas**, **Filtrar por valor**, the notices and the selection bar follow inside it. Reason: the table, its counter and its filters read as one unit under their own heading. The table shows the combination (a link to the detail; the web chevron is decorative so each row is one keyboard stop), sale price, stock (editable only in unsaved rows) and, in unsaved rows, a trash icon. An inactive row shows its price as text and cannot be selected; it is edited from its detail. Rows follow the saved order; new rows of a saved product stay together at the end until saved. The counter counts every variant except those marked for removal («6 de 100 variantes · 1 desactivada»); the final «Stock total» row sums the active variants. A saved product lists its uncreated combinations («1 combinación sin crear: S / Azul», or «12 combinaciones sin crear» with **Ver**, 50 at a time), each with **Agregar**, disabled at the limit. On mobile the «Variantes» screen holds the copied-stock notice, the inactive-source notice, the uncreated combinations and each row's rejection; going back keeps pending edits, and a save rejected for variant errors shows «2 con errores» on the product page.
- **Bulk editing:** a value chip is a toggle that selects the active, unmarked variants with that value; several active chips add up, and **Todas** selects everything or clears it. An active chip shows a check icon and is announced as selected. **Filtrar por valor** (web) shows only rows with that value and limits chips and **Todas** to visible rows, so «L» filtered plus the «Azul» chip selects L / Azul; selections hidden by the filter are counted in the bar and still edited. The selection bar offers **Editar precio**, **Editar stock**, **Editar precio de compra** (on mobile in the **⋮** menu), **Quitar** (unsaved rows only) and **Quitar selección**. Bulk edits replace each selected variant's value and stay pending until saving. **Editar stock** applies only to new variants and is disabled when all selected are saved. The web dialog says «Aplicar a las 3 seleccionadas.»; the mobile sheet adds the selected chips («(Azul)»).
- **Marks:** «Nueva» (not yet saved), «Desactivada», «Se quitará» (an unsaved thing the seller removes) and «Se eliminará» (a saved thing deleted on save), shown as text, not only color. One gesture removes values, options and combinations in new and saved products: the item stays visible, struck through with an inline **Deshacer** until saving, and out of totals, the counter and bulk selection. Rows marked by their value or option are undone from that value or option in the editor. In a saved product only «Nueva» rows have a trash icon; saved variants are deleted from their detail. A value whose variants have orders or stock is not marked and says «No se puede eliminar «Azul»: 2 variantes tienen pedidos o stock. Desactívalas desde cada variante.».
- **Variant detail** (web `/products/:productId/variants/:variantId`, mobile full screen): exists only for products with options; the route of a product without options leads to the product page. It shows the combination (read-only, with **Cambiar combinación**), sale price, purchase price, read-only stock, SKU, the QR with **Imprimir**, **Desactivar variante** / **Activar variante** and **Eliminar variante**, or the first reason it cannot be deleted (orders, then stock, then only variant). **Guardar cambios** saves only that variant's prices and SKU, also for an inactive variant; it is where the SKU of a saved variant is edited. Changing the combination, deactivating, activating and deleting are saved when confirmed (web dialog, mobile native alert). While an action waits, every action is disabled and the pressed one shows «Guardando…», «Desactivando…», «Activando…» or «Eliminando…». A missing variant shows «Esta variante ya no existe.» with **Volver al producto** when the product exists in the company, otherwise **Volver a productos**.
- **Unsaved variant detail** (a dialog on web, a screen on mobile): edits the row's prices, stock and SKU, says the QR is created on save and offers **Quitar**; **Listo** applies, **Cancelar** does not.
- **Reordering:** web options and values move by dragging or with the arrow keys on the focused handle. Mobile has no drag in this version: the handle opens a menu with **Subir** and **Bajar**, and screen readers get the same two actions. Reason: a long-press drag conflicts with scrolling and is not accessible; the menu is the same on both platforms for screen readers.
- **Photos:** the «Fotos» field (web) and the photo strip (mobile) of the create form and the product page, described in [Photos](#photos).
- **Printing:** the label carries the product name, the combination, the SKU and the QR; if the content does not fit it is not printed. Mobile (Android, Brother printer) prints from the print sheet of the product page, which lists each variant by combination, marks inactive ones and can print them, one variant per send with its copies; opened from a variant detail it comes with that variant selected. Web prints the label of a variant from its detail through the browser print dialog; web has no label for products without options.
- **Discard confirmation:** both platforms ask «¿Descartar cambios?» («Se perderán los cambios que no guardaste.», **Descartar** / **Seguir editando**) before leaving or cancelling with changes, before opening a saved variant detail from a product page with changes, before **Recargar producto** or **Recargar variante** with changes, and on **X** of «Opciones»; photos count as a change when the list to save differs from the loaded one or any photo is uploading or failed; closing or reloading the browser tab shows the browser's own warning. Nothing is kept as a draft.
- **Nueva venta:** offers only active variants and products with at least one, hidden or not, with options and values in saved order. An item whose variant was deactivated while the sale was being built returns to review marked «L / Azul ya no está disponible.».

### Combination text

- The variant name, its detail title, the print sheet, the label and the unavailable-item message use the combination: values in saved order separated by « / » («L / Azul»).
- «Nueva venta», the cart, the checkout and the order detail keep each platform's format over the attributes copied into the item, in option order: web «M · Azul», mobile «Talla: M · Color: Azul».

### Vocabulary

- **Eliminar** deletes a saved variant, value or option and has conditions; **quitar** discards something not yet saved. Both are marked with **Deshacer** until saving, except in the variant detail, where they apply at once.
- **Precio de compra** and **Costo** are the same field: web says «Precio de compra», the compact mobile field says «Costo».
- The product never says «atributos» in the interface; it is the technical name of the combination copied into orders.

## Hiding a product

A seller can hide a product with one switch, «Ocultar producto», so that it is not offered to buyers while she keeps selling it herself. Visibility is stored on the product (`Product.hidden`, default `false`) and is the only per-product visibility; it is not a status, because the status means the product exists for the seller and every seller path keys on it.

Two readings of "can be sold" are kept apart in code and in this documentation:

- **Sellable:** the seller can sell it (active product with an active variant, hidden or not). It is the rule of «Nueva venta», the point of sale, the order editor, the sale catalog search and every QR lookup.
- **Offered:** sellable and not hidden. It is the rule of every buyer-initiated read: the [public catalog](public-catalog.md), its cart and **Continuar**, the «Apariencia de la tienda» preview, the products [message-to-order](message-to-order.md) adds from texts and photos, the catalog search readiness and the «en el catálogo» of the [AI assistants](assistants.md).

Where each reading lives in code: `isOffered` (`domain/product-visibility.ts`) is the single TypeScript definition, repeated in SQL as `status = 'active' AND NOT hidden AND EXISTS (active variant)`. The sellable reads (`findSellableVariants`, `findSellableVariantsByQrCodes`, `searchSaleCatalog`, `findSellableVariantStock`) take no visibility filter and keep their meaning; new names for the offered reading use `offered` (`findOfferedVariants`, `isOffered`). `findOfferedVariants`, which serves the catalog cart refresh and **Continuar**, is exposed through `products/composition.ts`, not the `products/index.ts` barrel, which [architecture](architecture.md) treats as debt not to extend. Visibility is a stored boolean rather than a status value or a separate table: the status is keyed by every seller path, and visibility is an attribute with no identity or history (only the current value is kept, not when or who hid it).

### Hiding rules

| Aspect | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| What hides | The whole product, all its variants. Visibility is per product, not per variant; deactivating a variant already exists. Reason: hiding answers "do not offer this product". |
| One switch | It covers the catalog and message reading together (rejected: two switches). Reason: both are buyer-initiated sales and the seller's reason to hide one almost always applies to the other. |
| Default | Visible. A new product and every product that existed before the feature are visible. |
| Who changes it | Any user of the company who can edit products, on web or mobile. Hiding and showing need no confirmation and can be repeated any number of times. |
| What a hidden product leaves | For every read after the save commits: the public catalog grid, its search, the product page by direct URL and the carts holding it; the preview cards; the products message-to-order adds from texts, photos (QR and visual search) and pending completions; and the assistants' «En el catálogo» line and customer link. A page or a message already being read is not interrupted. |
| What a hidden product keeps | The seller's list (marked «Oculto»), its product page and variants, «Nueva venta» on web and mobile, the point of sale (its QR is read), the order editor picker, printing and every existing order. A hidden product is offered there with no mark: in a sale the seller makes, visibility to buyers does not matter. |
| Showing it again | Turning the switch off and saving returns it to the catalog and to message reading at once, with nothing else to redo. |
| Stock, prices, variants, photos | Hiding changes none of them. Stock is neither reserved nor released. Photos stay indexed, so a product shown again is recognized by photo at once. |
| Appearance preview | The «Catálogo» view of «Apariencia de la tienda» leaves hidden products out. Core returns no cards for a store whose products are all hidden, and the web route turns an empty list into the sample products with «Datos de ejemplo», never hidden products ([Checkout · Appearance](checkout.md#appearance)). |
| Orders already created | Never change: catalog, chat and manual orders that contain the product are shown, edited, paid, shipped and cancelled as before, and their checkout links keep working. |
| What a save writes | A product save writes the visibility only when the form changed the switch since it loaded, like photos; otherwise the stored value is kept whatever the form shows. Creating always writes it. Toggling the switch and back counts as unchanged. After the save the form shows the stored value. Reason: a form opened before another device hid the product must not silently show it again when it saves a description typo, and visibility, unlike prices, is buyer-facing. Writing it never raises `structureVersion`, so it never causes `PRODUCT_CHANGED`. |
| Two devices | Both change the switch: last save wins. One changes it and the other saves another field from an old form: the product stays hidden, and the second form shows the switch on after its save, with no extra message. |

### Product form

- The switch is a compact control at the top corner of the product form, like the status of Shopify's product page, with the short label «Ocultar producto» (pt-BR: «Ocultar produto») and no explanatory text, because hiding is simple and sellers understand it (founder decision, 2026-11-01). On the web, from the `lg` width up, it sits at the top of the right column, right above «Fotos», with the left column starting directly under the page header; below `lg` it is the first element of the form, right-aligned, above «Datos del producto», so the stacked right column does not bury it; there is no empty row for it, and it stays inside the form; on mobile it is a right-aligned row at the top of the form, above the photo strip, with the native switch and a touch target of at least 44 pt. It appears on create and on edit, for products with or without options and with or without active variants, and its accessible name is «Ocultar producto».
- The label always keeps the noun. Reason: the Variants card has an «Ocultar» / «Ver» button that collapses a list and «Apariencia de la tienda» has **Ocultar catálogo**; the switch never shortens to «Ocultar», and no text says «archivado», «invisible» or «catálogo oculto» for a hidden product.
- Accessibility: the label is associated with the switch (clicking it toggles), there is no description or hint (mobile: the state is announced as on or off), Space toggles it on web, and it is the first control of the form in the tab order.
- It is part of the form draft: it saves with **Guardar cambios** / **Guardar**, counts as a change for «¿Descartar cambios?» and the browser's leave warning, is restored by **Descartar**, and is disabled with the rest of the form while it reads «Guardando…». Creating a hidden product opens the new product's page with the switch on, as every create does (web with «Producto guardado correctamente.»; mobile with no alert).
- The save outcomes are those of [When something fails](#when-something-fails): a rejection saves nothing, visibility included, and the switch keeps the seller's position; offline and in-doubt saves keep the stored visibility until a save succeeds.

### Last-visible notice

When a save turned «Ocultar producto» on and took a published catalog from showing at least one product to showing none, the saved confirmation adds «Tu catálogo publicado se quedó sin productos visibles. Tus clientes verán la tienda vacía.» (pt-BR: «Seu catálogo publicado ficou sem produtos visíveis. Seus clientes verão a loja vazia.»). On web it follows «Producto guardado correctamente.» in the same message; on mobile it is a second paragraph of the «Cambios guardados» alert. It informs and neither blocks nor asks for confirmation, and once dismissed no persistent cue remains besides the «Oculto» marks and the buyer's empty catalog.

- It is not shown when the catalog is not published, when it was already empty, when the hidden product had no active variant or is in a currency other than the store's (the catalog did not change), or when the product is created hidden.
- Emptying the catalog by deleting variants in the form or deactivating them shows no notice. Reason: the product save cannot deactivate variants (variant status has its own action), so a notice for "any save" would cover deleting variants in the form but not deactivating them one click away; the notice is tied to hiding.
- Core decides it: the save reports whether it hid the company's last offered product of its currency, and the presentation layer turns that into the notice only when the storefront is published and the currency is the store's. A failed storefront read gives no notice and logs the failure; the save stays a success. Two simultaneous hides of the last two offered products can both miss the notice; accepted, since the empty catalog is still visible.

## Photos

A product has up to 8 photos in an ordered list. The first is the **primary** («Principal»): the photo shown wherever a single photo of the product is shown. A product with photos always has a primary; a product without photos has none and each surface shows its placeholder.

### Photo rules

| Aspect | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Limit | Up to 8 photos per product (`productPhotoLimits` in `shared/contracts/products.ts`), on create and edit, web and mobile. Core rejects a save with more from any client. Reason: founder decision; enough for front, back, worn and size chart without turning the product into an album. |
| Files | Each photo follows the [image rules](images.md#rules): JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 10 MB, 8,000 px per side and 24 MP. Mobile re-encodes to JPEG before uploading. Weight, dimensions and format are different texts for the seller. |
| Order | Photos keep the order in which they were added to the form: the picker's order inside one selection, then the order of later selections, not the order in which uploads finish. A thumbnail takes its place when chosen, and a failed photo keeps it, so **Reintentar** keeps the intended order. There is no reordering by drag or by moving up and down. Reason: founder decision; **Hacer principal** covers the one order that matters to buyers. |
| Primary | The first photo, with no separate stored mark (rejected: a primary flag, which can disagree with the order). In the form, the first thumbnail not marked for removal, whatever its state (kept, uploading or failed), so «Principal» never jumps when an upload finishes or fails. |
| Hacer principal | Moves a kept, unmarked photo to the first place; the others keep their relative order: [A, B, C, D] → **Hacer principal** on C → [C, A, B, D]. It is the only way to change positions and never applies to uploading, failed or marked photos. No confirmation. |
| Removing | Any photo can be removed. It stays in its place, dimmed and marked «Se quitará» (added in this session) or «Se eliminará» (already saved), with **Deshacer** until saving, the same pattern as values, options and combinations. Removing the primary makes the next unmarked photo the primary at once, without asking. A marked photo cannot be made primary; **Deshacer** returns it to its place, and to primary when it is then the first unmarked one. On save, marked photos leave and the rest close up in order; removing every photo leaves the product without photos. Reason: a mistaken removal must not cost the other edits. |
| Places | Every thumbnail in the form (kept, uploading, failed or marked) takes one of the 8 places until it leaves or the product is saved; a marked photo frees its place only after saving. |
| Uploading and failed photos | Never saved: saving waits until no thumbnail is uploading or failed. **Cancelar subida** and **Quitar** on a failed photo take it out of the field at once, without a mark, because it was never part of the product. |
| Uploads | Each photo uploads on its own through the image API; each client runs at most 2 photo jobs at a time (on mobile a job is preparing plus uploading, so at most 2 photos are decoded at once) and the rest wait as «Subiendo…». An upload unanswered after 2 minutes, counted from the start of its request, becomes a failed photo with **Reintentar**. A late answer for a cancelled or expired upload changes nothing; its orphaned image is harmless. Both values are one shared client constant (`photoUploadSettings`), not environment variables. Reason: they run in the web bundle and the mobile build, which have no per-environment configuration, and one constant keeps both platforms identical; changing them needs a release. |
| Saving | Photos, their order and removals are saved with the rest of the form, all or nothing; nothing about photos is saved before. |
| Photos only when touched | A save changes the photos only when the list it would save (which photos and in which order) differs from the loaded one; any sequence of actions that ends at the loaded list counts as untouched. A form that did not touch the photos leaves them as they are in core, even if another device changed them meanwhile. Reason: avoids silently losing photos added from another device (rejected: overwriting the list with every save, as name and description work). |
| Repeated photo | A save that lists the same photo twice is rejected like an unusable photo, with the form intact. The forms never send it. Reason: a repeat would take two places and make the primary and the marks ambiguous (rejected: counting it once). |
| Duplicates | The same file uploaded twice is two different photos and counts twice; there is no duplicate detection. |
| Removed files | Removing a photo takes it off the product and out of [visual search](image-product-search.md); it does not delete the file, whose public address keeps working ([Images](images.md)). «Se eliminará» means removed from the product, not erased. |
| Current, not copied | Orders copy name, combination, SKU and price only; every view shows the current primary, so changing it changes the thumbnail of open checkout links ([Checkout](checkout.md#product-thumbnails)). Reason: photos are presentation. |
| Where the primary is shown | Seller product list thumbnail on mobile (dashed placeholder with the photo icon when there is none), [public catalog](public-catalog.md) grid card (never a carousel) and cart, [checkout](checkout.md#product-thumbnails) product thumbnails and the «Apariencia de la tienda» preview cards. No seller order screen shows a product photo. The full ordered list is shown only in the seller form and in the public product page gallery; there is no gallery on the seller's product page. Reason: founder decision. |
| Parity | Web and mobile apply the same limit, order, primary rule, marks and texts through the shared field model `shared/product-photos.ts`, except the place of the hint (see below). Core is the source of truth. |
| Concurrency | Photo changes are part of the product save, written under the product row lock; they update the product but never `structureVersion`. Between two forms that both changed the photos, the last save wins. Reason: reordering or removing photos never invalidates another form's variants. |

### Thumbnails

Each thumbnail, on both platforms, is in one of these states:

| State | Shows | Actions | Accessible name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Uploading | The photo dimmed with a spinner and «Subiendo…» | **Cancelar subida** | «Foto 4 de 5, subiendo» |
| Kept | The photo | **Hacer principal** (not on the primary), **Quitar foto** | «Foto 3 de 5» |
| Marked | The photo dimmed with «Se quitará» or «Se eliminará» as text | **Deshacer** | «Foto 3 de 5, se quitará al guardar» / «…, se eliminará al guardar» |
| Failed | The photo dimmed when the device can show it (otherwise a neutral block), a warning icon and «No se subió» | **Reintentar** (only when retrying can work) and **Quitar** | Web adds the file name and the reason: «Foto 4 de 5, grande.jpg, no se subió: pesa más de 10 MB»; mobile adds the full reason: «Foto 4 de 5, no se subió: no se pudo subir. Revisa la conexión» |

- The primary carries «Principal» and its accessible name adds «principal» («Foto 1 de 5, principal, subiendo»). Reason: the seller sees which photo the catalog uses without remembering a rule, **Hacer principal** needs a visible counterpart, and screen readers get the same information; the label stores nothing.
- State texts sit on a solid surface with the field's colors, never as bare text over the photo, so they read on any photo.
- Positions («3 de 5») count every thumbnail in the field, whatever its state, in visual order.

### «Fotos» field (web)

- **The box:** a grid of 4 equal columns of square cells filling the width of the form's right column (full width when the column stacks on a phone), 4 columns × 3 rows. The photo at the first position of the list always takes the 2×2 cell, whatever its state (marked, uploading or failed), so the box height never changes; the «Principal» badge stays on the current primary, the first unmarked photo, even when that is a 1×1 tile after the first photos were marked; the other photos are 1×1 cells in order. The box reserves the whole grid, so its height is the same with 1 or 8 photos and never grows or shifts as photos come and go; free cells show nothing except the first one, the **Agregar fotos** tile (a dashed square button that opens the file picker). Without photos the 2×2 primary cell itself is the dashed **Agregar fotos** tile and carries the hint «Hasta 8 fotos. La primera es la principal.»; the box keeps its full height even then, so the form below does not jump when the first photo arrives. At 8 photos the tile is gone and one cell stays empty.
- **Actions on the photo:** no text or button sits under a thumbnail. Each action is an icon button (32 px) in the top-right corner of the tile: a star for **Hacer principal** (not on the primary, nor on an uploading, failed or marked photo), the trash for quitar, an undo arrow for **Deshacer** on a marked photo, an x for **Cancelar subida** and a retry arrow for **Reintentar** (when retryable) on an uploading or failed photo. They appear on hover and whenever focus is inside the tile, and are always visible on touch and other pointers without hover (`@media (hover: none)`). Each keeps its accessible name (below) and a visible focus ring, and its tooltip is the visible verb («Hacer principal», «Deshacer», «Cancelar subida», «Reintentar»; the trash repeats «Quitar la foto 3 de 5»).
- **States stay inside the tile:** «Principal» top-left; «Subiendo…», «No se subió», «Se quitará» and «Se eliminará» as chips at the bottom; the photo dims as before. On a 1×1 tile (about 68 px in the form column) the chip carries text only, at the field's smallest text size and wrapping only between words, so every word shows whole in Spanish and Portuguese; the 2×2 primary tile keeps the spinner or warning icon. A failed tile carries its reason in its accessible name; a rejected tile is described (`aria-describedby`) by its own text, never by whatever the shared status line shows.
- **One status line under the box**, in this precedence: (1) the save-blocking or validation message (unchanged behavior and focus); (2) the transient selection notice; (3) the reason of each failed photo («Foto 2 de 3: grande.jpg. Pesa más de 10 MB.», file name truncated in the middle) and each rejected photo («Foto 2 de 3: No pudimos usar esta foto. Quítala o súbela de nuevo.») in the error color; (4) the counter «3 de 8 fotos», with marks «5 de 8 fotos · 1 se eliminará» (when any marked photo was saved) or «· 1 se quitará». At 8 places the counter carries the limit: «8 de 8 fotos. Llegaste al máximo.», and with marks «8 de 8 fotos · 1 se eliminará. Guarda para liberar lugar.». Without photos there is no line: the tile holds the hint.
- **Agregar fotos** opens the file picker with multiple selection and stays available while uploads run. A selection over the free places adds the first ones and says at once, before any upload ends, «Elegiste 4 fotos y quedan 2 lugares. Se agregan las 2 primeras.» (singular «queda 1 lugar. Se agrega la primera.») until the next selection or save. At 8 places the tile disappears and the status line says «8 de 8 fotos. Llegaste al máximo.».
- A file over 10 MB fails at once with the weight text and is never sent; any `413 IMAGE_TOO_LARGE` for a sent file is therefore the dimensions text. Reason: core answers the same code for both limits and the browser knows the file size before uploading, so the contract does not change.
- Accessible names of the icon buttons carry the position: «Hacer principal la foto 3 de 5», «Deshacer quitar la foto 3 de 5», «Cancelar la subida de la foto 4 de 5», «Reintentar la foto 4 de 5».
- A failed thumbnail shows only «No se subió» over the photo; the file name and the full reason go to the status line, and the tile's accessible name still carries both.
- **Photo viewer:** pressing a tile opens the photo large in a modal. Every tile that shows an image opens it: saved, new and marked ones. An uploading or failed photo has nothing to show, so it has no open button. The open button covers the whole tile, sits under the icon buttons (they keep working and stay first in tab order) and cues hover with a tint and a ring; its accessible name is «Ver la foto 3 de 5». The title is «Foto 3 de 5», numbered by the photo's place in the whole field exactly as its tile is, so skipping uploading or failed photos does not renumber the rest. A hidden description says «Usa las flechas del teclado para ver las demás fotos.».
- **Stepping:** with several viewable photos the viewer has **Foto anterior** and **Foto siguiente** buttons and answers the left and right arrow keys; past either end it wraps around to the other. With a single viewable photo there are no buttons and the arrow keys do nothing, and the photo is centered. The photos stepped through are those with an image, in the order of the field.
- **Image area:** the image area has a fixed height so nothing moves while a photo loads, and the photo fits inside it. Below the `sm` breakpoint (phones) the previous and next buttons go under the image, so it keeps the dialog's whole width; from `sm` up they sit on either side. If a photo cannot load the area shows «No se pudo cargar la foto.» with **Reintentar**; stepping to another photo starts a fresh load. The orders receipt dialog shows its image with the same component and the same failure and retry behavior ([Orders · Payments](orders.md#payments)).
- **Closing:** **Cerrar foto**, Escape or a click outside close the viewer, and focus returns to the open button of the photo last shown, which may differ from the one opened after stepping. Reason: the seller continues from the tile they were looking at.
- Reason: ecommerce admins (Shopify, Tiendanube, Etsy, Amazon) use a fixed grid with actions on the photo; text under each thumbnail made rows uneven. Rejected for now: drag to reorder (left for a later task; **Hacer principal** stays the only way to change order).
- **Guardar producto** / **Guardar cambios** stays enabled while photos upload or failed. Pressing it sends nothing, says next to the field «Espera a que terminen de subir las fotos.» or «Reintenta o quita las fotos que no se subieron.», scrolls the field into view and moves focus to the first uploading or failed thumbnail's first action. Reason: a disabled button does not say why.

Focus and announcements (one polite live region for the field; the same text twice in a row is announced twice):

| Action | Focus goes to | Announcement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hacer principal** | **Quitar foto** of the moved thumbnail, now first | «Foto 1 de 5, ahora es la principal.» |
| **Quitar foto** | **Deshacer** of the same thumbnail | «Foto 3 de 5 se quitará al guardar.» / «… se eliminará al guardar.»; when it was the primary, adds «La foto 2 ahora es la principal.» |
| **Deshacer** | **Quitar foto** of the same thumbnail | «Foto 3 de 5 se conservará.»; adds «Ahora es la principal.» when it is primary again |

| **Reintentar** | **Cancelar subida** of the same thumbnail | None |
| Upload finished or failed | Does not move, unless focus was on that thumbnail's **Cancelar subida**; then it goes to the thumbnail's first action. Reason: the button under focus disappears and focus must not fall to the page | Failures only: «La foto 4 no se subió: pesa más de 10 MB.» |

- The strip keeps the form's compact height. ## When something fails

- **Known rejection** (repeated combination, limit, empty name, SKU in use, missing price, deletion not allowed): the form stays intact, each error is shown next to its field, option or row, with a summary on top. A rejected deletion (an order arrived meanwhile) shows «No se puede eliminar «Azul»: ahora tiene pedidos o stock.» and the seller undoes the mark.
- **Not sent** (offline): «No se pudo guardar. Revisa tu conexión e inténtalo otra vez.» (or the action's own text) with the form intact and **Reintentar**. On web a request is not sent when the browser reports no connection or the fetch itself fails; on mobile when the device has no connection.
- **In doubt** (sent, outcome unknown: the answer was lost or a server or gateway error came back): never shown as success. Creating says «No se pudo confirmar si se creó este producto. Revisa el catálogo antes de iniciar otra creación.» and a retry reuses the product id; saving or a variant action offers **Recargar producto** or **Recargar variante**. A retry while offline after an in-doubt attempt keeps the in-doubt warning. Reason: offline means nothing reached core, while an unknown outcome may have been applied.
- **Product changed** (`PRODUCT_CHANGED`): «Este producto cambió desde que lo abriste. Recarga el producto para ver los cambios.» over the intact form, with **Recargar producto**. Reason: the same seller triggers it when retrying their own save after a dropped connection, so the text does not blame another person.
- Web loads the full route manifest at start (React Router route discovery `initial`). Reason: lazy discovery fetched the manifest on submit, so an offline action failed before reaching its own handling and lost the form.
- A failed print on mobile shows the printer messages and changes nothing.

## API

`/api/products` requires a session (Bearer token for mobile, cookie for web) and an active company. Responses carry `Cache-Control: no-store`. Request bodies are strict (unknown fields are rejected); responses are tolerant and may gain optional fields, which shipped clients ignore. A variant status other than `active` is shown as inactive. Reason: a later field or status does not break an installed build.

| Operation | Route | Success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List | `GET /api/products?search=&page=&pageSize=` (page size default 20, max 100) | `200 { items, page, pageSize, total }` |
| Detail | `GET /api/products/:productId` | `200 { product }` |
| Create | `POST /api/products` | `201 { id }` |
| Save the editor | `PUT /api/products/:productId` | `200` detail |
| Edit a variant's price and SKU | `PATCH /api/products/:productId/variants/:variantId` | `200` detail |
| Change a combination | `PUT /api/products/:productId/variants/:variantId/combination` | `200` detail |
| Set a variant's status | `PUT /api/products/:productId/variants/:variantId/status` | `200` detail |
| Delete a variant | `DELETE /api/products/:productId/variants/:variantId` | `200` detail |

- There is no product-level `PATCH`: the editor sends the whole desired state with `PUT`, and single-variant fields go through the variant routes. Reason: partial product patches cannot express option and variant changes atomically.
- **Create** takes the ordered options (with client-generated UUIDs for options and values) and the variants as lists of value ids. A product without options sends `options: []` and one variant with `values: []`. Mobile generates the product UUID; core generates variant ids and QR codes. A second `POST` with an id already used returns `409 PRODUCT_ID_CONFLICT`, whatever the body. Reason: a retry after a lost response cannot create a duplicate product. Mobile shows the conflict and offers to review the list.
- **Save** carries the structure version, the full ordered options (an option omitted from the list is deleted; a new option for a product with variants names the value existing variants receive), the price and SKU of the saved variants whose price or SKU the form edits, the new variants, and the ids of deleted variants. Saved variants change combination only by adding an option or by deleting an option left with a single value; any other combination change goes through the variant route. A saved value can only be deleted together with all its saved variants. Core applies a diff in bulk with a fixed number of statements, whatever the variant count.
- **Hidden** is a boolean in the create request (required), the save request (optional: omitted means visibility untouched) and the detail and list responses. The `PUT` response is the detail plus `catalogEmptied` (the [last-visible notice](#last-visible-notice)). Editor clients build the body with the shared visibility helper (`shared/product-visibility.ts`), which sends `hidden` only when the form value differs from the loaded one. The contracts changed in place.
- **Photos** are uploaded first through the image API; create and save send only image ids. Create takes `photoIds` (ordered, `[]` for none). Save takes an optional `photoIds`: omitted means photos untouched, present means the whole new ordered list (`[]` removes every photo); clients send it only when the list to save differs from the loaded one. The detail returns `product.photos` (`{ id, url }`, ordered, primary first); list items carry `image`, the primary. Core checks the limit and repeats in the domain and each id against the company's images (a photo of another company is reported as missing), so the client gets the reason and the index into the sent `photoIds`: `TOO_MANY_PHOTOS` (scope product, with `maximum`), `DUPLICATE_PHOTO` and `PHOTO_NOT_FOUND` (scope photo, with `index`), reported together with every other issue. Reason: the client marks the exact thumbnail instead of a generic field error. A detail read whose photo cannot be resolved fails as `503 IMAGE_NOT_FOUND` instead of hiding the photo, because a later save would send the list without it.
- Every write answers the full product detail built after the write, with `hasOrders` per variant, so clients replace their state without another read.
- Errors use the shared `{ code, error }` envelope; field errors add `issues` with scope (product, option, value, variant or new variant), field, position and typed reason, so the client chooses the visible message by code and reason, never by the `error` text. All issues detectable together are reported together. Status mapping, codes and reasons are defined in `shared/contracts/products.ts` and `apps/core/src/features/products/presentation/api-errors.ts`.
- Ids that belong to another product or are repeated in the request are `422` issues with reason `ID_CONFLICT`; a primary-key race at write time is `409 ID_CONFLICT`.
- Mobile writes are online only and never retried automatically; after an uncertain result the screen offers **Recargar producto** or **Recargar variante**, and repeating status, combination (including one with a new value) or delete has no further effect.

## Concurrency

- There are no edit locks. Every structural write (create, a save that changes options, values or variants, changing a combination, deleting a variant) increases `structureVersion`. A save sent with an older version is rejected with `409 PRODUCT_CHANGED` and writes nothing; the form stays intact and offers **Recargar producto**. Prices, SKUs, photos, visibility and status changes do not increase it, so they stay last-write-wins. Reason: concurrent editing of one product is rare, but an editor working from a stale structure would delete or recreate other people's variants (rejected: reloading and reapplying the seller's edits automatically, which needs merge rules the product does not define).
- Writes lock the product row, so two actions on one product run one after the other. Name uniqueness, contiguous positions and per-product counts are checked under that lock, not by the database. Reason: a unique expression index cannot be deferred, so a reorder or name swap inside one save would trip it. Combination uniqueness is a unique index on a key built from the value ids, which renames and reorders do not change.
- The statement count of a create, a save or a variant action does not grow with the variant count; an integration test compares 2 and 100 variants. Reason: the product row lock stays held for milliseconds, not seconds.
- Editing transactions have an explicit timeout (`PRODUCT_WRITE_TRANSACTION_TIMEOUT_MS`, default 10 s) with a lower PostgreSQL lock and statement timeout; a timeout or lock conflict answers `503 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` and is safe to retry.
- A save that changes saved combinations locks those variants in id order, and orders insert their items in variant order, so the two cannot deadlock.
- Reads that issue several queries (the product detail with `hasOrders`, the catalog product, sellable lookups) run in one read snapshot, so a write committing between their queries cannot surface as inconsistent data.
- Deactivating a variant and creating an order in the same instant are not serialized: the order is equivalent to one created just before.
- «Has orders» is a lookup owned by orders and supplied to products by `src/features/products/product-editing.ts`. The final guard against deleting a variant that has orders is the database foreign key from order items to variants, which restricts deletion; a variant deleted while an order is being created makes that order fail as `VARIANT_NOT_FOUND` ([Orders](orders.md)).

## Observability

Writes log `product_created` (with `hidden`) and `product_saved` (with `photoCount`, and `photosChanged`, `hiddenChanged`, `hidden` and `hidLastOffered` on save), `product_catalog_emptied` when the notice applies and `product_catalog_emptied_check_failed` (warning) when the storefront read failed, `product_variant_updated`, `product_variant_combination_changed`, `product_variant_status_changed` and `product_variant_deleted` after commit, and `product_save_rejected` / `product_variant_action_rejected` for business rejections. Technical failures log `product_save_failed` or `product_variant_action_failed` with the stage once (`transaction_timeout` or `lock_conflict` for timeouts and deadlocks), and a failed reload after a committed variant action logs `product_variant_action_reload_failed`. Logs never contain names, SKUs or prices.
