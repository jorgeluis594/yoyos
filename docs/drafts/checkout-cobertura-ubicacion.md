# Checkout location limited to delivery coverage: product definition

**Status:** definition ready to implement. Adjusts the location fields of the public checkout's delivery choice ([Checkout · Delivery choice](../checkout.md#delivery-choice)) using the coverage rules of [Delivery](../delivery.md#zones-and-options), and aligns the seller's agency coverage warning in settings (web and mobile) with the same rule. Todoist task 6hrqR5Rf2J2pMV23, requested on 30/10/2026. The design and implementation plan goes in [checkout-cobertura-ubicacion-technical.md](checkout-cobertura-ubicacion-technical.md), written next and landed with this draft before promotion. Closed after five review-gate rounds (rounds 1 to 4: UX/UI approved, product rejected; the founder decided question 4 between rounds 3 and 4; round 5: both approved).

## Problem and situation

A buyer opens the order link the seller sent by WhatsApp, chooses «Entrega a domicilio» and looks for their place in Departamento, Provincia and Distrito. The comboboxes offer all of Peru: 25 departments, 196 provinces and 1,892 districts. The buyer picks Lima → Canta → Huamantanga, waits for the quotation and only then reads «No hay opciones de envío para este distrito. Elige otro distrito.». They have no way to know which districts would work, so they try others at random or write to the seller in the chat to ask where the store delivers. A seller who only delivers in Lima Metropolitana answers that question again and again, and the buyer sometimes abandons the order. A buyer who arrived from the store's public catalog has it worse: their order has no saved delivery, nobody talked to them about coverage, and they may never have written to the store. In addition, a seller with home delivery switched on but no home zone shows «Entrega a domicilio» to every buyer, and every district they pick ends in the same message.

## Goal

The buyer only sees the departments, provinces and districts where the store can deliver with the method they chose, so every district they can pick gives at least one home rate or one agency in the list. Hypothesis: fewer quotations that end without rates and fewer «¿hasta dónde envían?» questions in the chat; it would show in the share of buyer quotation requests (those authorized by an order link on the brand host) that return no rate, which should fall to near zero. Stored quotations do not tell a buyer's from a seller's, and sellers keep quoting uncovered districts on purpose, so the measure is taken on the buyer's requests; how they are logged is resolved in implementation. The agency dead end of today (a district quoted with an agency rate but with no agency in its department, which ends in «No hay agencias disponibles…») returns a rate, so it is measured separately: the share of buyer agency quotations whose agencies are all outside the district's department, which should also fall to near zero. Quotations started by preselection count like any other: they are buyer requests for an offered place, and they can only come back empty when coverage changed after the page loaded.

## Users and surfaces

| User | Need | Surface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer who opened the order link the seller sent in the chat and is choosing home or agency delivery | Find their place among the ones the store covers, without guessing | Public checkout on core web, mostly from a phone |
| Buyer who built a cart in the store's public catalog and tapped **Continuar** | The same, with no saved delivery and possibly no earlier conversation with the store; the catalog and the checkout have no contact button by decision ([Public catalog](../public-catalog.md)) | The same public checkout, on the store's brand host |
| Seller assigning a delivery to an order from the order screen | Choose the destination district and a rate | Web and mobile order delivery forms; not changed by this task |
| Seller setting up delivery in Configuración → Entregas | Know when a switched-on method reaches no buyer | Web and mobile settings; only the agency coverage warning changes |

## First-version scope

- The public checkout's Departamento, Provincia and Distrito comboboxes offer only the places covered by the chosen method, «Entrega a domicilio» or «Envío a agencia».
- «Entrega a domicilio» is offered only when home delivery is on and has coverage, as «Envío a agencia» already is.
- A single available choice at any level is preselected, and the buyer is told.
- The dead ends left (no method at all, a place not in the list) tell the buyer to check with the store, without naming a channel or assuming an earlier conversation, so the text is right for both buyer origins.
- The seller's warning «Falta una opción de agencia activa con cobertura» under the agency switch, on web and mobile, uses the same agency coverage rule as the checkout, so it appears whenever «Envío a agencia» would not reach any buyer. Its text, place and **Ir a zonas y tarifas** link do not change, and no other part of the settings changes.
- The server's quotation and confirmation stay exactly as today, for home and for agency: the department bound for agency applies to what the checkout offers and to the seller's warning, not to the server. The kept delivery, the rates, the agency cascade, the order snapshot and the rest of the seller's settings do not change.
- Meeting point with the decoupling of the location selector: the location control receives the places it may offer from outside and does not know about zones, methods or rates. Two Todoist tasks describe that decoupling and are likely duplicates: «Desacoplar el selector de distrito de Perú» (6hqc452cgxP3RF83) and «Refactorizar selector de distritos a DistrictSelector configurable por país» (6hhgPHPcVvfJH253). Whichever is kept only has to keep accepting that restriction; this task does not rename or restructure the control.

## Out of this version

- Filtering the seller's delivery forms on web and mobile. The seller knows their coverage, keeps the full country and learns there is no rate from the quotation, as today. Filtering only web would break web/mobile parity, and the mobile seller selector is a different control that the decoupling task will replace.
- A seller signal for partial agency gaps: a zone «Sur» = Arequipa, Cusco and Puno whose agencies are all in Arequipa keeps agency coverage (Arequipa), so no warning appears, while Cusco and Puno silently drop out of the buyer's list. Deferred risk, accepted by the product reviewer in the founder's place; the founder decides whether to create a Todoist task: the seller loses agency buyers in those departments without knowing it. Today those buyers reach «No hay agencias disponibles…»; after this change those departments are not offered. Left out because a per-department signal is a new message in the zone editor on web and mobile, and a zone spanning several departments with agencies in only some of them may be intended. Trigger: a seller reports buyers who could not find their department under agency, or the founder asks for the Todoist task.
- Explaining the agency warning's new case: the warning «Falta una opción de agencia activa con cobertura» now also appears when the zone, the option and an agency are all enabled but every agency sits outside the departments the zone covers (criterion 27); the text does not say so, and the seller sees everything enabled in the zone editor. Deferred together with the partial-gap signal, for the founder to decide both at once: the text, place and link are kept by decision, and the zone editor shows the agencies and covered places. Trigger: the same as the partial-gap signal.
- Showing the buyer a list or map of the store's coverage outside the comboboxes: not needed while the comboboxes already show it; revisit if buyers keep asking in the chat.
- Updating the offered places while the page is open: the places are those of the moment the page loaded. A seller who adds a zone is seen after a reload. Not a real risk: the server still quotes and confirms with its own rules (see Server).
- Offering an «I am not in the list» path (asking the seller for a quote) or a contact button: the buyer who came from a chat solves it there; the catalog and the checkout have no contact button by decision («No WhatsApp contact button» in [Public catalog](../public-catalog.md)). The page only says to check with the store. Known limit: a catalog buyer outside coverage who has no other way to reach the store cannot complete the order; this changes only if the catalog or checkout gets a contact, which is a separate decision.

## Flow

1. The buyer opens the checkout. The delivery methods offered are «Conservar entrega actual» (with a saved delivery), «Entrega a domicilio» (home on and covered), «Envío a agencia» (agency on and covered) and «Recojo en tienda · Gratis», in this order. The preselected method is the saved delivery, otherwise the first offered method. This changes one case: a store with home on but without home coverage used to open on «Entrega a domicilio»; it now opens on «Envío a agencia» when agency is covered, otherwise on «Recojo en tienda · Gratis».
2. With «Entrega a domicilio» or «Envío a agencia» chosen, Departamento lists only the departments with at least one district covered by that method. If there is only one, it is preselected.
3. After a department, Provincia lists only its provinces with a covered district; one alone is preselected.
4. After a province, Distrito lists only its covered districts; one alone is preselected, and the quotation starts as soon as a district is set, exactly as when the buyer picks it. A preselected district stays visible in its field above the address, the notice says it was filled, and after confirming «Datos del comprador» names the address and district as today, so a buyer who lives elsewhere can notice it.
5. The buyer continues as today: a home rate and address, or an agency from the cascade and the identity document, then confirms.

Alternative paths:

- **Entering home or agency from any other method** (home ↔ agency, store pickup → home or agency, «Conservar entrega actual» → home or agency, **Pasar a Entrega a domicilio** from the no-agency message): one rule. The current district stays if the new method covers it, and it is quoted again. If not, the district is cleared, the department and province stay only if the new method covers them, the preselection of steps 2 to 4 applies, and the buyer sees the location notice (see Screens). The current district is always the last state of the form's district: the one the buyer last picked under home or agency, otherwise the saved delivery's district, otherwise none. An explicit clear counts as the last state: if the buyer cleared the district, it stays empty and the saved delivery's district does not come back; the department and province left on the form are kept if covered, and entering the method runs preselection again, because it is a new location event (a level cleared in an earlier visit to the method can therefore be filled again; within the same visit a cleared level is not filled again until the buyer gives an upper level a new value, which runs preselection below as in «Reselecting an upper level»). Example: the buyer picks Miraflores under home, taps «Conservar entrega actual» (saved district Surco), then goes back to «Entrega a domicilio»: the starting point is Miraflores. Without a pick in this visit, it is Surco. A saved store pickup has no district, so without a pick the location starts empty with preselection. This replaces the docs/checkout.md rule «Changing method clears rate, agency and quotation but keeps the district»: rate, agency and quotation are still cleared, and the district is kept only when covered.
- **Changing department or province:** clears the levels below, as today; then a single remaining choice below is preselected.
- **Clearing a preselected level:** the level stays empty until the buyer picks again; it is never refilled on its own, and the levels below are cleared as with any change. Example: Lima → Lima → Miraflores were preselected; the buyer clears Distrito: it stays empty, rates disappear, and picking Miraflores again quotes it.
- **Reselecting an upper level:** choosing the value an upper level already has changes nothing. Choosing a new value (including picking a department again after clearing it) runs preselection for the levels below.
- **Kept delivery whose district is no longer covered, or whose method is now hidden:** «Conservar entrega actual» is still offered and preselected with its summary, rate and amount; confirming with it is accepted.
- **Coverage removed after the page loaded:** the buyer picks a district that was offered, the quotation comes back empty and they see the existing messages («No hay opciones de envío para este distrito…» for home, «No hay agencias disponibles para envío a …» with **Cambiar distrito** for agency).

## Screens

**Checkout → Entrega (location fields of «Entrega a domicilio» and «Envío a agencia»):**

- Same three comboboxes, layout, search (no case or accents, match in bold) and clearing rules as today. The ubigeo is still never shown or searchable. Preselected values keep their field label and the same visible focus ring as any value.
- **Hint:** one hint for the whole location group, right under the group title «Distrito de entrega» and before Departamento, always shown whatever the size of the coverage (even when the store covers all of Peru): «Escribe para buscar. Solo aparecen los lugares donde la tienda entrega; si el tuyo no está, consulta con la tienda.». It keeps teaching that the buyer can type, and screen readers read it as the description of Departamento only, the first field, so it is not repeated while tabbing through the three. It replaces, in the checkout only, the hint that sits under Distrito today. It names no channel and does not assume the buyer already talked to the store, so it fits a buyer from the chat and one from the public catalog.
- **Empty search** (typing a name that matches nothing in the open list), shown inside the list. These texts are the checkout's own; the seller's location fields keep «No hay resultados. Prueba con otro nombre.». It does not claim the store does not deliver there, because the cause may be a typo («Mirafloes»); the hint above already says only covered places appear:
  - Departamento: «Ningún departamento de la lista coincide. Revisa cómo lo escribiste.»
  - Provincia: «Ninguna provincia de la lista coincide. Revisa cómo lo escribiste.»
  - Distrito: «Ningún distrito de la lista coincide. Revisa cómo lo escribiste.»
- **Location notice:** one notice space in the «Entrega» section, right under the delivery methods and above the location fields, outside the part that appears and disappears with home or agency. It is on the page whenever the delivery section is (empty when there is nothing to say), its text is replaced inside it, and while empty it takes no space and leaves no gap between the methods and the fields. It is a block distinct from the hint (see Emphasis); on a phone a cleared-district message stacks above the group title and the hint, and implementation checks that stack on a narrow screen with the keyboard closed. It shows at most one message, chosen by what the last location event did. A location event is: the location fields appearing (page load or entering home or agency) or an upper level getting a new value.

  | What the event did | Message |
  | --- | --- |
  | Cleared the buyer's district and preselected nothing | «Envío a agencia no llega a Miraflores. Elige otro distrito.» |
  | Cleared the buyer's district and preselected some levels, district still empty | «Envío a agencia no llega a Miraflores. Completamos los campos que tienen una sola opción; elige tu distrito.» |
  | Cleared the buyer's district and preselected a district in its place | «Envío a agencia no llega a Miraflores. Completamos los campos que tienen una sola opción.» |
  | Preselected one or more levels, nothing cleared | «Completamos automáticamente los campos que tienen una sola opción.» |
  | Neither | Empty |

  The method is named as in the method list and the district always as the comboboxes spell it, in title case («Entrega a domicilio no llega a Huamantanga. …»), whatever its source. The message changes or empties on the next location event; the buyer picking or clearing a field without triggering preselection empties it; changing to «Recojo en tienda» or «Conservar entrega actual» hides the location fields and empties the notice. The wording is the same when the cleared district came from the saved delivery rather than from a pick («Entrega a domicilio no llega a Huamantanga. …»): for a saved home delivery the buyer has just seen it in the «Conservar entrega actual» summary (in the catalog's uppercase, «HUAMANTANGA»); a saved agency delivery's summary shows the agency, not the district, so there the notice is the first place that names it, which is enough to explain the cleared field. «Elige otro distrito» is kept even when the department or province was cleared too, since the district is what the buyer ends up choosing. A message never asks for something already done: when preselection fills the district, the text does not say «Elige otro distrito».
- **Announcements:** the notice is read by screen readers once per change, including when it is filled by entering home or agency from another method, politely, never moves focus, and is announced before the quotation status «Consultando opciones de envío…», which keeps its own space; neither drops the other. Both spaces exist before their text appears so the change is announced reliably.
- **Emphasis:** a message that reports a cleared district (the first three rows) is shown as a block with padding, like «No hay opciones de envío para este distrito…», so it is not missed on a phone; the preselection-only message uses the hint's look. Neither relies on color alone.
- **Themes:** the hint, the notice and the empty-search texts are styled by the themes like the existing hint and status blocks (small, muted text; the muted block for the cleared-district messages) in the five checkout themes (Clásico, Boutique, Antojo, Suave and Noche), with legible contrast on each, Noche included.
- **No method available** (no covered home, no covered agency, no store pickup, no saved delivery): «No hay modalidades de entrega disponibles. Consulta con la tienda cómo recibir tu pedido.». It replaces the existing es and pt texts of that message.
- **Portuguese** (the checkout's other language):
  - Hint: «Digite para buscar. Só aparecem os lugares onde a loja entrega; se o seu não estiver, consulte a loja.»
  - Empty search: «Nenhum departamento da lista corresponde. Confira como você escreveu.», «Nenhuma província da lista corresponde. Confira como você escreveu.», «Nenhum distrito da lista corresponde. Confira como você escreveu.»
  - Notice: «Envio para agência não chega a Miraflores. Escolha outro distrito.», «Envio para agência não chega a Miraflores. Preenchemos os campos que têm uma só opção; escolha seu distrito.», «Envio para agência não chega a Miraflores. Preenchemos os campos que têm uma só opção.», «Preenchemos automaticamente os campos que têm uma só opção.» (with «Entrega em domicílio» for home)
  - No method: «Não há modalidades de entrega disponíveis. Consulte a loja sobre como receber seu pedido.»

The seller's location fields keep their current hint, empty text and full country list.

**Configuración → Entregas (seller, web and mobile):** the agency switch shows «Falta una opción de agencia activa con cobertura» with **Ir a zonas y tarifas** while agency is on and no district has agency coverage under the rule below (for example, a zone that covers only Cusco whose agencies are all in Arequipa). The text, place and update moments (when zones are saved, enabled or disabled) are the current ones; the home warning is unchanged.

## Rules

| Aspect | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Home coverage of a district | Home delivery is on and an enabled zone that covers the district (it lists the district, its province or its department) has an enabled home option. |
| Agency coverage of a district | Agency delivery is on and an enabled zone that covers the district has an enabled agency option with at least one eligible agency (enabled agency, enabled catalog courier, enabled company courier) located in that district's department. Reason: the agency list never shows agencies outside the buyer's department, so a district without one there would end in «No hay agencias disponibles…». |
| Agency coverage of the store | At least one district has agency coverage under the rule above; equivalently, an enabled zone has an enabled agency option with an eligible agency located in a department the zone covers in whole or in part. A zone that covers only Cusco with agencies only in Arequipa gives no agency coverage at all. This one meaning is used by the checkout and by the seller's settings warning, and replaces the docs/delivery.md coverage rule for agency (line 18); the quotation and assignment rules (lines 75 and 83) stay as they are. |
| Agency method offered | «Envío a agencia» is offered only when the store has agency coverage. |
| Per method | Each method offers its own places. Example: zone «Lima Metropolitana» = province Lima with home option «Motorizado» S/ 10.00; zone «Sur» = departments Arequipa, Cusco and Puno with agency option «Shalom» S/ 8.00 whose only agencies are in Arequipa. With «Entrega a domicilio» the buyer sees only Lima → Lima → its 43 districts; with «Envío a agencia», only Arequipa → its 8 provinces → their districts; Cusco and Puno are not offered. |
| Whole department in a zone | The department, all its provinces and all their districts are offered (for agency, if the department has an eligible agency of the option), including districts the catalog adds later. |
| Whole province in a zone | Its department is offered with only that province (plus any other covered one), and all its districts. |
| Loose districts | Their department and province are offered with only those districts. A zone with only Miraflores (150122) offers Lima → Lima → Miraflores, all three preselected, and the quotation starts at once. |
| Several zones | Places are the union of every zone that gives the method coverage; overlap shows each place once. |
| Disabled zone or option | Gives no coverage; its places are not offered unless another enabled zone covers them. |
| Hiding a method | «Entrega a domicilio» is offered only with home coverage somewhere, «Envío a agencia» only with agency coverage somewhere. A switched-on method without coverage is not shown to the buyer, and the seller sees the «Falta una opción … activa con cobertura» warning in settings on web and mobile, which now uses the same coverage rule for agency. **Pasar a Entrega a domicilio** in the no-agency message appears only when «Entrega a domicilio» is offered, so it is absent when home is on but uncovered. |
| Preselection | Runs when the location fields appear (page load or entering home or agency) and when an upper level gets a new value: a level with exactly one choice is set. Several choices: nothing is preselected at that level. Clearing a level never triggers it. The buyer is told through the location notice. |
| Entering a location method | The district is kept only if the new method covers it; otherwise it is cleared and the location notice names it. Rate, agency and quotation are always cleared. The current district is the last state of the form's district (last picked under home or agency, else the saved delivery's; an explicit clear leaves it empty). This replaces the docs/checkout.md rule «Changing method clears rate, agency and quotation but keeps the district». |
| Initial method | The saved delivery, otherwise the first offered method. A store with home on but uncovered now opens on «Envío a agencia» (if covered) or «Recojo en tienda · Gratis», no longer on «Entrega a domicilio». |
| Recovery texts | The hint and the no-method text say to check with the store, never name a channel or assume an earlier conversation, so they hold for a buyer from the chat and one from the public catalog. No contact button or flow is added. |
| Moment | The places and the hidden methods are decided when the checkout loads, like agency coverage today. |
| What the buyer learns | Only which places the store delivers to by each method. Zone names, options, prices, couriers and agencies are not part of it; rates and agencies still come only from the quotation of a chosen district. This replaces the docs/checkout.md rule that the public checkout receives coverage only as a yes/no flag. |
| Server | Quotation and confirmation stay exactly as today. For home, a district outside home coverage gets no home rate and its confirmation is rejected. For agency, the server keeps the zone and option rule (docs/delivery.md, Quotation and Assigning delivery): a district in a zone with an agency option that has an eligible agency anywhere still gets an agency rate, and a confirmation with an agency associated with that option is accepted even if the agency is outside the district's department. So a request made outside the page (for example, quoting Cusco directly in criterion 6's store) can still get an agency rate and confirm it with an Arequipa agency, as today; the page never offers it. No money or isolation risk: the price is the option's and the agency belongs to the store's option. The department bound applies only to the places the checkout offers, to whether «Envío a agencia» is offered, and to the seller's warning. |
| Kept delivery | Is an agreement already recorded in the order: it is offered and confirmed without checking current coverage or whether its method is still offered, as today. |

## When something fails

The task adds no action with effect: choosing a place is local and the existing quotation and confirmation keep their four outcomes.

| Situation | What the user sees | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The checkout cannot load its delivery options | The page's existing load error; nothing is shown as «no coverage» | Reload the page |
| No method available | «No hay modalidades de entrega disponibles. Consulta con la tienda cómo recibir tu pedido.» | Reach the store by whatever means they already have; the page offers no contact |
| The buyer's place is not in the list, or the name was mistyped | The empty-search text of the level («Ningún distrito de la lista coincide. Revisa cómo lo escribiste.») and the group hint («Escribe para buscar. Solo aparecen los lugares donde la tienda entrega; si el tuyo no está, consulta con la tienda.») | Fix the spelling, or check with the store |
| Picked district lost coverage after loading (home) | «No hay opciones de envío para este distrito. Elige otro distrito.» (or «… o recojo en tienda.») | Choose another district or method |
| Picked district lost coverage after loading (agency) | «No hay agencias disponibles para envío a …» with **Cambiar distrito** and, when home is offered, **Pasar a Entrega a domicilio** | Same as today |
| Quotation fails or does not answer | «No se pudieron cargar las tarifas. Tus datos siguen aquí.» with **Reintentar tarifas** | Retry; the chosen place is kept |
| Confirming a rate that stopped applying | The existing «Revisa la entrega y vuelve a confirmar.»; the form keeps its values and the delivery is quoted again | Choose again and confirm |
| Buyer leaves and reopens the link | Starts again from the saved delivery (if any) and the places of the new load | — |

## Who sees and does what

| Actor | Can see | Can do | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer with the order link (from the chat or from the public catalog) | The places covered by each offered method of that order's store | Choose a covered place, quote it, confirm | See zone names, options or prices other than the quotation of the chosen district; see agencies not returned by that quotation; see another store's coverage |
| Seller | Their full settings and coverage warnings, which match what buyers are offered (agency included) | Configure zones as today | Get a signal for departments of a zone left without an agency (out of this version) |

Risk named: a competitor with an order link learns where the store delivers, and for agency, which departments have an agency the store ships to. That is already learnable today by quoting districts one by one through the same link, agencies are public courier locations, and the store advertises its coverage to every buyer anyway; prices and zone layout stay out.

## Information that is kept

Nothing new is stored. The offered places are computed at each load and are ephemeral. The order keeps the same delivery snapshot as today, and a kept delivery keeps its destination even if it is no longer covered.

## Vocabulary

- **Coverage:** the places a method delivers to, given by the enabled zones and their enabled options (for agency, with an eligible agency in the place's department). Not the price (rate) and not the method itself. A method «has coverage» when at least one place is covered. This meaning governs what the buyer's checkout offers and the seller's settings warning. The server's quotation and confirmation keep the zone and option rule for agency (an eligible agency anywhere), which is not called coverage.
- **Offered places:** the departments, provinces and districts the checkout's location fields list for the chosen method.

## Closed decisions

| Decision | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Coverage is per method in the checkout | A district covered only for home would end in «No hay agencias…» under agency, the same dead end this task removes. |
| Agency coverage requires an eligible agency in the district's department | The agency list never goes outside the buyer's department; without this, a covered district could show no agency. |
| Home without coverage is hidden, like agency | Every district would end in «No hay opciones de envío…»; the seller is warned in settings. |
| The seller's agency warning uses the department-bounded rule on web and mobile | A hidden «Envío a agencia» must never go unnoticed by the seller (founder, 30/10/2026). |

## Questions decided by the review gate

Answers from the review gate of the implement-task skill, for the founder to confirm in the final pull request.

1. **Exposing covered places to the public checkout.** Decided by the UX/UI reviewer and the product reviewer (both agreed from round 1), consolidated by the coordinator: expose, per method, only the covered departments, provinces and districts, loaded with the page; never zone names, options, prices or agencies; agency places follow the department-bounded rule (question 3). Reason: it is the minimum the filter needs, and the same places are already discoverable by quoting district by district with the order link. Discarded: keeping the yes/no flag and only changing the empty-quotation message, which leaves the buyer finding out after picking.
2. **Seller delivery forms.** Decided by the UX/UI reviewer and the product reviewer (both agreed from round 1), consolidated by the coordinator: out of this task on web and mobile. Reason: the seller knows their coverage and quoting a gap is informative; web-only filtering breaks parity; the mobile seller selector belongs to the decoupling task. Discarded: filtering web and mobile (overlaps the decoupling task) and web only (breaks parity).
3. **Agency coverage and the agency cascade.** Decided by the coordinator after the product reviewer's round-1 blocker: a district has agency coverage only when an enabled zone covering it has an enabled agency option with at least one eligible agency in that district's department. Reason: keeps the goal true, since the cascade never shows agencies outside the buyer's department. Discarded: counting any eligible agency and accepting a «No hay agencias disponibles…» dead end named in the failures.
4. **Seller agency coverage warning.** Decided by the founder on 30/10/2026, after the product reviewer's round-3 blocker: the settings warning «Falta una opción de agencia activa con cobertura» on web and mobile adopts the same department-bounded agency coverage rule, so the seller is warned whenever «Envío a agencia» would not reach buyers. Seller settings on web and mobile enter the scope for this warning only. Reason: a hidden «Envío a agencia» must never go unnoticed by the seller. Discarded: keeping a separate seller-facing meaning of coverage and accepting that «Envío a agencia» can disappear from the checkout with no signal to the seller.
5. **Server and the department bound for agency.** Decided by the coordinator on the product reviewer's recommendation, after the round-4 blocker: the server's quotation and confirmation stay exactly as today for agency (zone and option rule, eligible agency anywhere); the department bound applies only to what the checkout offers and to the seller's warning. Reason: keeps seller quoting unchanged and the scope small; a forged request outside the department carries no money or isolation risk. Discarded: adding the bound to the quotation and confirmation, which also changes what the seller gets when quoting.
6. **Signal for partial agency gaps and for the warning's new case.** Decided by the product reviewer in the founder's place in round 5, with the UX/UI reviewer asking to defer both together: no seller signal in this version; both are recorded in «Out of this version» as deferred risks for the founder to decide whether to create a Todoist task. Reason: the affected buyers already reach «No hay agencias disponibles…» today and now get the same outcome earlier with a hint to check with the store; a zone spanning departments with agencies in only some of them may be intended; a new message in the zone editor on web and mobile would widen the scope. Discarded: a per-department message in the zone editor, and rewording the warning (its text, place and link are kept by question 4).
