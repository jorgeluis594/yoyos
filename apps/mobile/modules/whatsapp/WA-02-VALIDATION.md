## Scope and contracts

The native Android and iOS writers publish authenticated `YOYOWA01` snapshots through `state.next` to `state.bin`, keep recovery and session keys separate, persist a trusted read budget and creation record, serialize mutations by revision, preserve pending recovery when the session is unusable, and retire session keys only after recording their removal. They are internal module writers; WA-03 will connect them to the Go protocol store and delivery callbacks. No real WhatsApp account or manual phone QA was used.
