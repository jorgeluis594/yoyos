# Product form v3 comps

Three HTML comps (`comps.html`) for the native product form, informed by the Shopify mobile "Add product" screen: close and save in the top bar, card sections, summary of pricing and inventory. PNGs are 3x Playwright renders of each phone in dark and light themes; they are decision mocks, not device captures.

The user selected **option A** (Shopify-style cards) on 2026-10-09.
